# Business Performance Analysis Using Tableau

## Project Overview

This project analyzes business performance using Tableau. The dashboard provides an interactive view of Sales, Profit, Quantity, Category, Region, Product Name, and Order Date.

## Dataset Fields

- Category
- Order Date
- Product Name
- Region
- Profit
- Quantity
- Sales

## Visualizations

1. Sales by Category – Bar Chart
2. Sales Trend Over Time – Line Chart
3. Sales by Region – Bar Chart
4. Profit by Category – Bar Chart

## Interactive Filters

1. Category Filter
2. Region Filter

## Key Findings

1. Sales performance differs between product categories.
2. Sales performance varies across different regions.
3. Profit differs between categories, and higher sales do not always result in higher profit.

## Recommendations

1. Focus more on high-performing categories and regions through targeted marketing and promotions.
2. Analyze low-profit categories and improve pricing, product selection, and sales strategies.

## Overall Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides an interactive overview of business performance using sales, profit, category, region, and time-based analysis. The visualizations and filters help identify high-performing and low-performing areas and support better business decision-making.

## Tools Used

- Tableau Public
- Microsoft Excel
- Business Sales Dataset

### Task 11

# Customer Shopping Analysis Using Tableau

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior using Tableau. The dashboard provides an interactive view of Category, Customer ID, Gender, Invoice Date, Invoice Number, Payment Method, Shopping Mall, Age, Price, and Quantity.

## Dataset Fields

- Category
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Invoice Date
- Invoice No
- Payment Method
- Shopping Mall
- Age
- Price
- Quantity

## Visualizations

1. Shopping Mall Sales – Bubble Chart
2. Customer Distribution by Gender – Pie Chart
3. Price Trend Over Time – Line Chart
4. Quantity by Shopping Mall – Bar Chart

## Interactive Filters

1. Category Filter
2. Shopping Mall Filter

## Key Findings

1. Customer purchasing patterns vary across different shopping mall categories.
2. The distribution of customers differs between male and female customers.
3. The quantity purchased varies across different shopping malls, showing differences in customer demand.

## Recommendations

1. Shopping malls with higher customer demand should focus on maintaining product availability and improving customer service.
2. Businesses should use customer gender, category, and purchasing patterns to create targeted marketing campaigns and promotions.

## Overall Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides an interactive overview of customer shopping behavior using different visualizations. The bubble chart, pie chart, line chart, and bar chart help identify customer distribution, price trends, purchasing quantity, and shopping mall performance. These insights can support better marketing and business decisions.

- Tableau Public
- Microsoft Excel
- Customer Shopping Dataset
